# Tim Lytle — Brand Context

> The voice contract for timlytle.net. Read at the start of every `/seo` run. Drafted 2026-11-02 from the live homepage and talk pages; positioning confirmed by Tim ("both, equally").

## Product

- **Name:** Tim Lytle (handle: @tjlytle)
- **One-liner (≤20 words):** Engineering leader and backend architect who builds APIs, leads teams, and gives conference talks.
- **What it is:** A personal site. It does two jobs equally: get Tim hired for long-term advisory relationships (developer to CTO-level; long-term, not necessarily full-time) and show his conference speaking (26 talks, 51 deliveries since 2012, with public joind.in ratings).
- **Pricing structure:** n/a. The call to action is "Book a conversation" (Google Calendar link) or email.
- **Free tier?** n/a

## Audience

- **Primary personas:** (1) a CTO, founder or hiring manager looking for a hands-on engineering leader or API architect; (2) a conference organizer or attendee looking for a speaker or a talk's slides.
- **Jobs to be done:**
  1. "Can this person sort out our API / platform architecture or lead our team?"
  2. "Is this a good speaker for our conference, and what does he talk about?"
  3. "Where are the slides / abstract for the talk I just saw?"

## Positioning

> Tim Lytle is an **engineering leader and backend architect** for **teams building API-first systems** who need **someone who runs the org and still ships code**.
> He wins because **twenty-plus years of API work, plus a public, rated record of teaching it on stage**.
> He is **not** for **one-off, small contracts like "build and launch our WordPress / small-business website."** He works in **long-term client relationships** as an advisor, whether that's hands-on developer or CTO-level. Long-term, not necessarily full-time. (Confirmed by Tim, 2026-11-03.)

## Competitors

None in the product sense. The AEO problem is **entity disambiguation**: a plain search for "Tim Lytle" returns other people (attorneys, an actor named Timothy Lyle, genealogy pages). Success is engines resolving "Tim Lytle" + API / PHP / speaker to this site.

## Brand voice

- **Voice tags:** first person, plain, dry, self-deprecating, technical without jargon ("and a few yaks").
- **Person/perspective:** "I" on the site; third person ("Tim") only in machine-facing text (llms.txt, schema).
- **Forbidden words/phrases:** "seamlessly", "passionate", "rockstar", "ninja", "thought leader", "synergy".
- **Forbidden claims:** don't invent talk counts, ratings or employers; numbers come from `build/talks.json`.
- **Reference for tone:** the existing homepage hero and talk blurbs.

## Default author

Tim Lytle. Credentials: 20+ years building API-first systems; 26 conference talks; TEDx speaker; published technical author.
